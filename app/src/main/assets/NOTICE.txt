This is an independent Android companion for Herdr. It is not an official Herdr
application. Herdr is developed by its upstream contributors and runs separately
on the host. This application talks to Herdr's public API and terminal-session CLI.

Terminal rendering uses ConnectBot Terminal (org.connectbot:termlib), licensed
under Apache License 2.0, and its embedded libvterm, licensed under MIT.
The native camera preview uses AndroidX CameraX, licensed under Apache License
2.0. Offline pairing-code decoding uses ZXing Core (com.google.zxing:core),
licensed under Apache License 2.0. Camera images are not uploaded or saved.
AndroidX/Jetpack Compose, Kotlin, kotlinx.coroutines, kotlinx.serialization,
OkHttp and Okio are also licensed under Apache License 2.0.
The bundled Apache 2.0 and libvterm MIT texts are in assets/licenses.
See THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md and the bundled dependency licenses.

Native math rendering uses huarangmeng/latex 1.5.6 (MIT), with bundled
KaTeX 0.16.11 fonts (MIT). Their full notices are in assets/licenses.
